MY MESSAGE

Speaking with dignity.

I am a young person from Colombia, and I want my voice to be heard with respect. This site is a careful space to share my experience, seek guidance, and remind everyone that no young person should feel alone or unprotected.

WHAT MATTERS

Safety, respect, and support.

Being heard

Everyone deserves to speak about what they have experienced without blame or humiliation.

Protection

The safety and privacy of young people must come first.

Responsible support

Seeking help from a trusted adult and the appropriate authorities can open a safer path forward.

For safety, this site does not publish addresses, phone numbers, names of minors, private conversations, or intimate material. It does not encourage anyone to contact, pursue, or attack another person.
